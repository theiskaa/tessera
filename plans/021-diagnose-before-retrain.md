# Phase 021: Repair training data and selection before another detector run

Status: in progress. The private evidence report is `internal/reports/m7/v19c-root-cause-audit.md`. No further training is authorized by this phase until the gates below pass.

## Why this phase replaced the dose experiment

V19c gained 10.6 points of Georgian address found recall but lost 2.4 points of passing US address exact F1. Halving its dose in V20 could measure another point on a curve, but would not identify the cause. The user asked to diagnose and fix current quality problems before training again. V20 was already running when that correction arrived; the user interrupted its trainer and watcher during epoch 3. Preserve its files as an aborted diagnostic and do not use its synthetic validation as a release decision.

The verified defects are actionable: 27 groups of identical Georgian silver text have conflicting person/org labels; 161 duplicate rows beyond the first receive another tenfold draw; Georgian addresses are just 202/4,251 (4.8%) baseline silver address spans; adding 600 synthetic GE spans raises the share to 16.5%; the strict silver re-export still has unresolved issues; and checkpoint choice uses synthetic validation despite a large real-data gap. Five unlabeled village-only fields headed "address" are consistent with the existing annotation guide, which excludes a locality alone from address labels. The V17/V19c run also changed the shuffle of common examples and the learning-rate schedule because the draw count changed, so its US loss cannot be assigned uniquely to the new GE data.

## Work sequence and review gates

2. **Fail closed on duplicate supervision.** When loading silver, compare labels for identical text. Deduplicate copies with identical detector targets; reject conflicting targets with both source locations before GPU training. Test clean duplicates and conflicts. This guard is implemented in `trainer/src/detector.rs`; full review remains pending.
3. **Finish Phase 003 silver QA.** Adjudicate all GE address-bearing silver documents and the 200-document stratified sample, the 27 conflicting duplicate groups, and strict-export issues. Apply the existing guide's exclusion of locality-only fields without adding address labels to those five cases. Write a versioned clean silver input, with source/country/kind counts, immutable hashes, and no gold text. Verify the strict export and loader both pass. Record reviewed denominator and errors; do not infer a rate from structural flags.
4. **Fix the experiment and checkpoint decision.** Freeze a publisher-aware real development criterion that reports exact precision, recall, and F1 and address found recall per country; keep a fresh publisher set sealed for final confirmation. Make common-example sampling and the learning-rate schedule comparable between a future control and candidate, or explicitly quantify seed variance. A synthetic validation number alone cannot select a checkpoint.
5. **Only then consider training.** Use reviewed, source-diverse real GE contact examples and reviewed US mailing/identifier negatives if the error audit supports them. Freeze one intervention, input hashes, and country regression guards before a matched control/candidate run. No shipping until real contact ownership, parser, latency, and fresh-publisher gates pass.

## Data acquisition and label-quality gate

All 3,114 existing `data/raw/silver/ge-*` pages have IDs present in the odd-round silver exports, so there is no unused Georgian raw-page reserve to add to the current recipe. More data means collecting new source-diverse pages or improving labels on the current pages. The present QA worklist is 452 distinct text hashes: 27 duplicate-conflict groups, 125 remaining strict-export cases, and 300 additional distinct texts from the stratified semantic sample. Review the full document for each case, record missing, wrong-kind, boundary, and ambiguous labels separately, and adjudicate disagreements under the existing annotation guide. Independent second-pass review is required for disputed cases; agreement alone does not prove a shared label is correct. Preserve every original export and publish only a versioned, hash-bound cleaned silver file after review. This worklist is intentionally enriched for suspicious rows; its correction fraction is a triage yield, not an estimate of the full silver label-error rate. A separate frozen, country-stratified random audit packet now samples 100 unique texts per country from the unapproved deduplicated silver universe, records selection probabilities, and includes documents regardless of their current labels. Its 500 documents overlap the targeted packet in 15 texts. Report country denominators and uncertainty after independent review; source slices are exploratory because 100 per country does not guarantee enough cases per source. The sampling frame excludes the 27 quarantined conflicting texts, which must be adjudicated in the targeted packet. Keep this audit distinct from intervention selection and sealed evaluation.

The loader now refuses silver labels it cannot encode instead of silently treating their text as negative. The unapproved 12,185-document candidate currently fails this stricter preflight on 136 positive spans: 124 cross paragraph breaks, seven miss token boundaries, and five overlap rule-detected email/phone text. They occur in 54 documents; 45 are outside both existing QA packets. A separate blind 54-document packet and a location/reason worklist are private under `data/interim/silver/qa-unreachable-20260926/` and `internal/reports/m7/silver-unreachable-triage.jsonl`. Review them before any clean export. A structurally unreachable span may be a correct address that the current decoder cannot produce, so do not discard it solely to pass the gate.

After classifying defects in the targeted packet and estimating population error on the separate random sample, collect real Georgian contact/address pages from additional official publishers and formats under a verified **page-specific** source-licence basis. The broad `GE_GOV` shorthand requires a source-qualification audit before reuse; public-body status alone is insufficient evidence for every full web page. Split publishers into training, development, and sealed final sets **before** labeling or tuning; reject exact document overlap and evaluation-source overlap before a page enters training. Give special attention to numbered branch lists and full postal boundaries, while collecting reviewed US mailing-list and identifier negatives implicated by V19c's false positives. Count **distinct reachable** address spans and independent publishers, not repeated draws or synthetic templates. Do not set a synthetic dose or a minimum new-page count from the current accuracy scores alone; set the target from the measured label error rate, source coverage, and publisher-level uncertainty after QA.

## Frozen decision protocol for the next eligible run

This protocol is fixed before another run. The 877 repeatedly inspected real documents (gold SHA-256 `3940db21008a51dc71e78fdb390b1be3b1f69ca4596b4c49b50da0af77b2abbd`) are **development data**, suitable for checkpoint decisions but not final confirmation. The host-annotated copy (SHA-256 `e283b6db04469fc67945f0204a597fe25da092fa2f91062073325f1213028887`) has the same texts and labels. The primary mode is automatic country hint. The evaluator must report exact precision/recall/F1 and one-to-one address found recall by country, with URL host slices and verified publisher IDs once available. Synthetic validation remains a training diagnostic, never the sole checkpoint selector.

The future control and candidate must share synthetic shards, parser n-gram table, architecture, initialization seed, batch size, total draw count, example positions for common data, optimizer steps, and learning-rate schedule. A fixed draw manifest must prove these invariants before launch; one documented replacement of reviewed examples is the intervention. If this cannot be achieved, quantify run-to-run seed variance with a matched control before attributing a country change to the intervention.

Evaluate every completed epoch checkpoint with the same evaluator and quantization settings on the real development set. Reject an epoch if any previously passing primary person/address exact-F1 slice loses more than 2.0 percentage points versus V17. A Georgian-address intervention also needs at least +5.0 points of one-to-one found recall and +1.0 point of exact F1 versus V17. Among epochs satisfying both requirements, choose the lowest maximum shortfall against the frozen per-country targets (person exact F1 90, address exact F1 80, address found recall 95), then lowest sum of shortfalls, then the earliest epoch. If none passes, reject the run. Record all epochs and all slices; do not pick an epoch from synthetic macro F1.

The selected candidate needs a publisher robustness check before release: verify publisher organizations rather than treating URL hosts as publishers, show each publisher's contribution to the Georgian gain, and require the gain to remain positive after excluding the single largest contributing publisher. Evaluate once on a fresh sealed publisher set absent from both training and the repeatedly inspected development set. This final set must remain uninspected while selecting data, hyperparameters, and checkpoints. Real contact ownership, parser, and browser latency gates remain separate release conditions.

## Review after this phase

The phase is incomplete until the clean input and evaluation protocol are documented and all checks pass. The acceptance evidence must show the before/after silver composition, the exact disposition of every conflicting duplicate, labeled-source diversity, and no unresolved high-risk annotation cases. New model scores are not evidence that a data defect was fixed unless these inputs are first proven clean.
