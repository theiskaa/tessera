# Phase 013 review — V19 Georgian contact layouts

V19 was a diagnostic training run, not a release candidate. It added one deterministic silver file containing 800 Georgian contact-list documents with 2,400 distinct address spans from the parser **training** split. The effective V17 config differed only in run name and this one path. All 13 V17 silver files, parser table, architecture, seed, class weights, and four epochs remained fixed. The new docs were sampled without reusing an address within this supplement.

Independent checks verified 800 unique texts, 2,400 ordered nonoverlapping UTF-8 spans, no exact normalized overlap with GE gold address labels, no whole-document gold overlap, and six spaced samples for plausible address-list layouts. Rerunning the generator reproduced file SHA-256 `948e4d6ba406dbb520a514ca5ae4f0a78334056b8dcaeaa3007952fe041f1f1e` and config SHA-256 `b839dd4c59f712579ca5f24e794e50ad0ff6b158f18f2435f3ebfcd7e3530a2e`. The source parser-train SHA-256 is `14c14085ffafe56a6a780ec7fa683eecdffcb2f9706c7e5f1b5ab0ec9b94cd30`. The trainer loaded 153,998 synthetic, 9,042 validation, and 13,173 silver docs (15,118 pieces, 152,877 reachable spans, 136 unreachable) at startup. The +800 docs/+2,400 spans exactly match the supplement.

The deeper sample audit caught a concrete instance of the known OSM-derived noise risk: an unlabelled business name followed an otherwise labelled address and would have been included in the detector address target. V19 was interrupted at approximately step 1,200, before its first checkpoint, and remains an aborted run. The corrected V19b generator rejects all alphabetic material outside parser-labelled components; 70 of 3,288 eligible source rows fail this check. V19b was then interrupted at approximately step 8,200, also before a checkpoint: a second audit found that 2,400/2,400 address spans had commas and virtually none used `#`, `№`, or `N`, while 311 GE real development addresses often use those number markers and 42% lack a comma. The contact layout factor remained useful, but that narrow address form was not a sound training input.

V19c samples from the same parser training split with the stricter no-unlabelled-text gate and deterministically varies plain, `#`, `№`, and `N` house-number forms plus comma/space separators. A further audit reduced the dose from 800 to **200** contact-list documents: 600 new address labels repeated tenfold represent about 17% of combined GE address-label exposures, versus roughly 45% at the larger draft dose. The final filter excludes non-Georgian/non-Latin alphabetic text as well. Its file has 200 unique texts, 600 distinct correctly sliced address spans, no exact gold address/document overlap, and SHA-256 `84f64c8a7b6d4f628b6d1362bb69ba8ea2daee5c197405a991f493ddadc85183`. The effective config SHA-256 is `bf4d747a3fdd1ec0e8f7a714ba888ae0bed5b2d9ca576503e192bf05864eb1d4`, differing from V17 only in name and one supplemental path. Independent checks counted 150 assigned examples per number form, 361 address spans containing commas, UTF-8 nonoverlap, script purity, and ten inspected sample docs. A rerun reproduced file/config hashes. The ignored brief `internal/reports/m7/v19c-ge-contact-experiment.md` froze the expected GE gain, all 15 per-country guard slices, and source-host diagnostic.

V19c completed all four epochs and passed the artifact-bound int8 gate. On the frozen 877 real development documents, GE address found recall rose 57.2% to 67.8% (+10.6 points), with a paired URL-host interval [+2.2, +19.1]. GE address exact F1 rose only 35.7% to 37.6%; US address exact F1 fell 92.4% to 90.0%, breaching the predeclared >2-point passing-slice guard. A separately staged epoch-3 checkpoint also failed the guard, more severely for US and JP. The tiny synthetic contact fixture F1 rose 86.3% to 94.3%, but real ownership-annotated contact quality remains unknown. Two private post-result reviews, `internal/reports/m7/post-v19c-review-1-data.md` and `post-v19c-review-2-evaluation.md`, document the changed examples, score controls, host concentration, and epoch check. **Decision: retain V17 as internal reference; V19c is a completed diagnostic, not selectable or shipped.**
